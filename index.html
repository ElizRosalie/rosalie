About the Film

Twelve-year-old Rosalie's life looks like a fairy tale — idyllic, sheltered, and safe. But as every child knows, every fairy tale has a monster. When her family doesn't care to see the ogre in plain sight, she decides to take matters into her own, soon to be bloody, hands.
